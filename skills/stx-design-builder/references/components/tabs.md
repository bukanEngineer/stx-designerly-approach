# Secondary Tab

A compact tab control that switches between related sub-views within a page, used at a lower level of hierarchy than primary navigation.

---

## Guidelines

### When to use

- To organize content at a lower level of hierarchy within a page or a specific section.

- When navigating between related groups of content that share the same context.

- To provide a cleaner, more compact alternative to primary navigation bars.

### When not to use

- For a significant amount of content; take the user to a different page to utilize space a component can't provide.

- When there is only one category or piece of content to display.

- To represent a sequence of steps; use a Stepper or Progress Bar instead.

---

## Behavior

- Only one tab can be active at a time; selecting a new tab deactivates the previous one.

## Placement

- Horizontally centered in the viewport or container.

- Left-aligned or centered depending on the layout's primary content alignment.
