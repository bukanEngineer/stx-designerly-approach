# Modal

A centred overlay that blocks the page flow to confirm a non-revertible action or capture a single focused decision from the user, available in Default, Illustration, and New Feature variants.

---

## Guidelines

### When to use

- Serve as a single purpose dedicated to completing the user's task

- Require a user response from the system

- Confirm a non-revertible destructive action

### When not to use

- For a significant amount of content, take the user to a different page to utilize space a modal can‘t provide

## Behavior

- The height of the modal is determined by the content

- If modal content exceeds the height of the viewport and impedes a user’s ability to maintain context or see important actions, the content within the modal can be made scrollable

- A modal can be dismissed by: - Clicking on the  **Close**  button within it. - Clicking on a  **Cancel**  button (or similar) within it. - Pressing the  `Esc`  key. - Clicking outside of it

## Placement

- Opens on top of all other content with a transparent backdrop behind it.

- Top-aligned at the extra-small (xs) viewport size and otherwise vertically centered in the viewport.

- Horizontally centered in the viewport.
